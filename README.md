# Virtual Cemetery

Virtual Cemetery gives people a private place to plan ahead and gives families a lasting place to remember someone. A person can tell their stories in their own words, choose what others may see, and name someone they trust to care for their memorial.

Imagine your grandfather choosing a photograph and recording the story behind it. He can preview the page with you today. Years later, the person he chose can share that story with the family, and relatives can add memories of their own. His funeral guest list and estate wishes stay in the private part of the service.

## A first milestone

Test three screens with your grandfather and a prospective memorial steward using sample information. Ask him to add a memory, show you exactly who will see it, and choose who can care for it later. Ask the steward to explain what they will be able to read. If their answers differ from the permissions on screen, revise the design before collecting real family records.

This repository currently contains the design. The build plan starts with an invitation-only family pilot and adds outside services as their permissions, costs, and operating requirements become clear.
