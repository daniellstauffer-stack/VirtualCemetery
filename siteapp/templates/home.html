Two clearly separated spaces

Private workspace

My Plan

A private place for a living person. Record burial or cremation preferences, service wishes, emergency contacts, where important papers are kept, and memories you are considering sharing.

Nothing inside My Plan is ever searchable or visible to visitors. Every memory has its own clear audience setting.

Shared remembrance

The Memorial

A separate, lasting page with your chosen name, portrait, life story, selected gallery, and moderated guestbook. Preview it while you are alive so you know exactly what family will see.

Published only after a trained reviewer verifies a death report and gives the living owner a chance to challenge any error.

From a private plan to a verified memorial

Step 1 · Prepare

Choose stories & a steward

Select photos, confirm who may see each one, preview your memorial, and invite a trusted steward (“the person who manages my memorial”). Revoke access anytime.

Step 2 · Verify carefully

Human review, never guesswork

Inactivity never triggers a report. A trained staff reviewer checks independent evidence, contacts the owner directly, and pauses any conflicting claim for a second reviewer.

Step 3 · Share separately

Publish & organize on your schedule

Publishing a memorial, releasing designated funeral wishes, and sending funeral invitations are separate deliberate actions—never automated blasts.
